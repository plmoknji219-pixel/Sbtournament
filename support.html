How can I join a tournament?

Open the Tournament page, select a tournament and use the Join Match option.

Where can I see my match?

Open your Player Dashboard to check your joined matches.

What if my match room information is missing?

Contact support and provide your Game UID and tournament information.

What if I entered the wrong Game UID?

Send a support request with the correct information.
