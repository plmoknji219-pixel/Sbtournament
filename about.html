🎮 About SB Tournament

SB Tournament is a gaming tournament platform designed to make tournament registration, match participation and tournament information easier to manage.

Players can explore available tournaments, register for matches, check their match information and view leaderboard results.

✨ Our Features

  • 🏆 Tournament listing
  • 📝 Player registration
  • 🎮 Match joining
  • 🚪 Match room information
  • 📊 Player dashboard
  • 🥇 Leaderboard
  • 🆘 Support system

🛡️ Fair Play

SB Tournament supports fair and respectful competition. Players are expected to follow the published tournament rules and respect other participants.

📞 Support

If you need help with registration, matches or other tournament issues, please visit our Support page.
