SB Tournament

Rules & Guidelines

📜 Tournament Rules

  • Players must provide correct registration information.
  • Each player must use the registered Game UID.
  • Players should join the assigned match on time.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the tournament admin.
  • Do not intentionally disturb or exploit other players.

🎮 Match Rules

  • Check the match room information before joining.
  • Join only the match assigned to you.
  • Follow the official match schedule.
  • Report technical problems to Support as soon as possible.

🛡️ Fair Play

  • Cheating and unauthorized game modifications are not allowed.
  • Do not use unfair third-party tools or exploits.
  • Do not impersonate another player.
  • Respect other players and tournament staff.

⚠️ Disqualification

  • Rule violations may result in removal from a match.
  • Cheating or fraudulent activity may result in disqualification.
  • Final decisions should be made according to the tournament's published rules.

📞 Need Help?

If you have a problem with registration, a match, or your account, contact the SB Tournament support team.
